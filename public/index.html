Harvest scenarios

Explore collection scenarios

Qualitative examples for discussion. These are not measured regional risks or conservation assessments.

Root and bark collection

Consider the effect of removing roots or bark, the source of the material and recovery time. Field observations are required before assessing pressure.

Wild collection in sensitive habitats

Consider habitat condition, collection frequency and local knowledge. No occurrence or habitat dataset is integrated into this prototype.

Cultivation and traceability

Explore cultivated supply and documented sourcing. A lower impact cannot be assumed without comparing local practices and outcomes.

Data needed for an assessment

Verified species identity, licensed occurrence records, collection observations, habitat information and a documented validation method. No numerical pressure scores are reported.

RESPONSIBLE RESEARCH

How EthnoGuard handles knowledge

Every record separates cultural report, scientific evidence and inference.

01

Provenance first

Keep source, place, community context and access restrictions attached to every claim.

02

Evidence, not endorsement

Study type, replication, sample quality and relevance require review. This demo does not assign evidence scores.

03

Uncertainty visible

Show missing toxicity, dosage and interaction data instead of hiding gaps.

04

Community rights

Flag sensitive knowledge and support consent, attribution and benefit-sharing.

AI disclosure

This prototype was AI-assisted. Plant records are demonstration summaries and must be validated against primary literature before research or policy use.
